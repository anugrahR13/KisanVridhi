# KisanVridhi: Simple & Friendly Project Guide

---

## 🌾 What is KisanVridhi?

**KisanVridhi** (meaning *"Farmer Growth & Prosperity"*) is a smart mobile and web app designed to act as a **digital assistant and advisor for farmers**.

Imagine having a knowledgeable agricultural expert, a financial accountant, a weather reporter, and a market guide right inside your mobile phone 24/7. That is exactly what KisanVridhi does!

---

## ❓ Why Was KisanVridhi Created?

Most farmers face four big challenges every day:
1. **Unpredictable Weather**: Not knowing when it will rain leads to wasted water, electricity, and damaged crops.
2. **Rising Input Costs**: Farmers spend heavy money on seeds, fertilizers, and labor without knowing if they will break even at harvest time.
3. **Crop Diseases**: When leaves turn yellow or get spots, farmers often buy expensive chemical sprays without knowing the exact disease.
4. **Unfair Crop Prices**: Farmers often sell their crops at low prices because they don't know the daily rates in nearby markets (Mandis).

**KisanVridhi solves all these problems in one easy-to-use app.**

---

## 📱 Core Features Explained in Simple Words

### 1. ☀️ Today's Farm Check (Your Daily Morning Assistant)
Every morning when a farmer opens the app, they instantly see:
- Today's temperature and rain chances.
- **Smart Watering Advice**: Tells the farmer whether to water the field today or skip watering because rain is expected (saving water and electricity!).
- **Urgent Tasks**: Reminders for important farm jobs due today (like applying fertilizer or weeding).

---

### 2. 📸 Mobile Leaf Health Scanner (Crop Disease Detector)
- If a farmer notices yellow leaves, spots, or insects on their crop, they simply **take a photo using their mobile camera**.
- The app instantly scans the leaf photo and identifies the plant disease.
- It provides two clear solutions:
  - 🌿 **Organic Natural Remedy** (like neem oil or homemade mixtures).
  - 🧪 **Safe Chemical Spray** (if the disease is severe).
- **Human Expert Safety Check**: If the app is not 100% sure about the disease, it alerts the farmer and connects them directly with Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) agricultural experts.

---

### 3. 💰 Farm Expense & Profit Calculator (Digital Passbook)
- Farmers can type in whatever money they spend—on seeds, labor, tractor rental, or fertilizer.
- At harvest time, they enter how many bags of crop they harvested and the selling price.
- **Minimum Selling Price Calculator**: The app automatically tells the farmer the minimum price per kg they MUST sell at so they don't suffer a financial loss!

---

### 4. 🌦️ Smart Weather & Irrigation Guide
- Shows 5-day weather forecasts for the farmer's specific village or district.
- Helps farmers plan planting, spraying, and harvesting days so rain doesn't ruin their hard work.

---

### 5. 🏛️ Government Subsidy & Scheme Finder
- Keeps a simple directory of central and state government schemes (like PM-Kisan, drip irrigation subsidies, solar pump grants, and crop insurance).
- Shows who is eligible, what documents are needed (Aadhaar, Land proof), and how to apply.

---

### 6. 📊 Mandi Rates & Government Crop Buying Notices
- Displays today's live crop prices in nearby market yards (Mandis) so farmers get the best price for their hard work.
- Lists government Minimum Support Price (MSP) purchasing centers.

---

### 7. 📁 Secure Locker for Farm Documents
- A safe place inside the phone to store photos of land records, soil test reports, bill receipts, and insurance policies so they are never lost.

---

### 8. 🎁 Eco Missions & Seed Reward Discounts
- Farmers earn points (XP) whenever they adopt eco-friendly farming practices like drip irrigation, mulching, or organic composting.
- These points can be redeemed for real discount vouchers on seeds and bio-fertilizers.

---

### 9. 🤖 Ask KisanVridhi AI Friend
- A friendly voice and text chat assistant where farmers can ask questions like *"How do I make organic Jeevamrut?"* or *"When should I harvest my wheat?"* and receive simple, verified advice.

---

## 🚀 How a Farmer Uses KisanVridhi Step-by-Step

```
Step 1: Open KisanVridhi App on Mobile Phone
   │
Step 2: Check "Today's Farm Check" (Weather, Irrigation Advice & Tasks)
   │
Step 3: Spot a problem on a plant leaf? Take a photo for instant AI Diagnosis!
   │
Step 4: Spent money on fertilizer? Log it in the Expense Passbook.
   │

```
